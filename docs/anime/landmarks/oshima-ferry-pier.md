# The Oshima ferry piers: エースポート / PIER7 (inner bay) and 浦の浜 (大島)

## History and current state

- The **大島汽船 regular ferry between 気仙沼 and 大島 ended on 2019-04-07**, after 100 years, when the Oshima bridge
  opened.
  - It ran between **「エースポート」** on the inner bay and **浦の浜港** on 大島, and took about 25 minutes.
  - The last sailings used the **「フェリー亀山」**, which was sent back to Hiroshima afterwards.
- **Today (2026)**, 大島汽船 runs the **気仙沼ベイクルーズ**: 50 minutes under both bridges, from April 26 each season.
  - Departures from the **PIER7 pontoon** (南町海岸1-11): 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:30 and 14:30.
  - The Oshima calling point is the **浦の浜 ウエルカムターミナル pier**.
  - The boats are **「ファンタジー」 and 「やしま丸」**.
- エースポート itself was merged into PIER7 (see `pier7.md`).

## The vessels

- **ファンタジー** (Commons, August 2026):
  - A fast white passenger boat, about 25 to 30 m long, with 2 cabin decks, a mast and a radar arch.
  - Livery: a **deep blue bow swoosh #1f4fa8**, a **red panel #cc2b2b** at the stern, blue dots, a thin red boot-top.
  - The name in English script 「Fantasy」 and katakana 「ファンタジー」.
- **やしま丸**: the second cruise boat; no photo was checked.
- A **car ferry is no longer in service**.
