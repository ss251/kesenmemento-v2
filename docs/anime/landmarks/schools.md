## What the app renders

- `app/schools_core.jpg`: the school buildings exist as generic mid-zone lots (grey boxes, some with green roofs).
- The schoolyards are **covered in trees and forest crowns**. The land-cover classification treats the pale dirt
  fields as grass or forest, so the large open bare-earth yards, the pool and the running tracks are missing.
- No school gate, fence, pool, clock or school sign.
