## Structure and appearance

- Designed by **石山修武 (Osamu Ishiyama)**, opened **1994**, Architectural Institute of Japan Prize 1995.
- RC with partial steel. **3 levels plus a roof garden**, total 4,601 m².
- "Ark" means Noah's ark. The building reads like a **spaceship or ship**.
  - A long, low body clad in **ribbed aluminium-alloy panels** (#b9bdc2, weathered) and pinkish rendered walls
    (#c9a4a2), made with shipbuilding and aircraft techniques.
  - On the roof, **two salmon-pink boxy "pods" stand on thin steel stilts**, like lookout cabins (#e3a7a3; underside
    #b98580). A tall narrow concrete chimney or mast and antenna masts stand next to them.
  - A **wide roof deck** with a railing.
- Roof: ortho #d0dfe1 → light grey metal #cfd2d2, with a white vaulted/curved roof over the gallery (seen on the ortho).
- The museum's exhibitions are not modelled; the app draws the building only.
