# 神明崎 (Shinmeizaki peninsula)

## Position and shape (measured on the aerial photo and the DEM)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Neck (the road bend of 東浜街道 / 県道 気仙沼港線) | ≈ (340–380, −150); ground T.P. 5 to 6 m |
| Tip (浮見堂) | (341.6, −23.4), i.e. 38.90621, 141.57894 |
| Length, neck to tip | **about 130 m**, running N→S |
| Width | 45 to 55 m in the middle; the tip is a rounded 35 m nose |
| High point | T.P. **16.3 m** at (362, −130); the middle of the peninsula is T.P. 13.5 m |
| Sides | **West**: a wide sloping concrete revetment/quay (light grey, 10 to 25 m wide) that forms the east edge of the inner bay. **East**: a vertical seawall onto the 魚町 east quay basin, with a narrow walkway. The red **浮見海道** walkway rings the tip |

The app's `SPOTS.shinmeizaki` (352, −80, r 45) is consistent with this.

## Contents, north to south

1. The main torii at the road bend.
2. The 参道, then a steep stair up to **五十鈴神社** on the knoll (see `isuzu-jinja.md`).
3. A long single-storey building of about 8 × 28 m with a dark red-brown roof, on the middle of the ridge at
   (352, −72). It is the GSI footprint; it is probably the 社務所 or a hall, and OSM places a 社務所 node at (348, −90).
4. **猪狩神社** with its torii at (348, −52).
5. The tip, with **浮見堂**, the **立ち恵比寿像** (359, −22) and the walkway.

The whole knoll is covered in dense mixed forest (the モクゲンジ grove is a natural monument).

Colours from the aerial photo:

- Tree canopy #4f7a4a to #6d9a58 (the ortho median of the canopy is #658267).
- West revetment concrete #c4c6bf.
- Walkway vermilion #c9503a.

## Current state

The peninsula seawalls, the west revetment, the walkway and the 浮見堂 are all recent works, finished around
2019–2021. To the east, 魚町's inner-bay seawall (T.P. 4.1 m flap-gate type) ends at the peninsula neck.
