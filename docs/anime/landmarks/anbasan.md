## Structure and facilities

- The name expresses a wish for safe voyages and good catches (安らかな波; search summary of the tourism pages). It is the symbol of the port town,
  and people climb it for the first sunrise of the year (初日の出).
- **Three viewpoints on the path from the car park (20 spaces):**
  1. **ひのでのてらす (6合目)**, about 5 minutes from the car park.
  2. **ほしのてらす (8合目)**, about 10 minutes.
  3. The **summit**, about 15 to 20 minutes.
- **安波山公園 (5合目)** has a dragon theme: two dragons welcome you at the entrance, and there is a 「りゅうの階段」.
- The footlights up to the first viewpoint stay on until 22:00.
- **Summit (ortho):** a small **grassy clearing of about 20 × 25 m**, open to the south, surrounded by mixed forest.
  - No roofed pavilion or large deck is visible from above.
  - The sources mention "things sent from abroad" at the summit (monuments) but no gazebo.
  - A foot path descends south through a long cleared grassy strip (its purpose is not verified) toward the road
    switchbacks.
- The ortho shows mostly **dark conifer plantation (likely sugi)** with lighter broadleaf patches. The clearing below the
  summit is grass.
