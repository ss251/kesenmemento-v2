- The station building is a **long, narrow single-storey bar of 66.6 × 14.7 m** (OSM), 586 m², oriented about 40°, that
  is NE–SW, parallel to the tracks. The front faces SE onto the station square.
- The DEM gives T.P. 20.3 m. The station is in 古町, on the west side of the city, about 1.5 km from the inner bay.

## Structure and appearance (from photos, 2016–2026)

- **Single storey, timber structure**, renovated in 2012 on the theme 「漁港のまち」.
- **Facade:** an **arcade of five round arches** across the front, in **off-white stone-tile cladding** (#e2ddd3) with
  darker arch reveals. The glazed entrance is in the centre arches.
- **Roof:** a dark slate-grey metal hip/gable roof (#4f5763; ortho #81a1a2 → #7d8488). A **small centre gable with a
  triangular window** stands over the entrance.
  - The **front roof slope carries a large painted mural** of **swordfish (メカジキ) and fishermen fighting**, by a local
    artist. It is repainted over the years.
- The entrance has an **approach gate modelled on three Sanriku-coast rocks** (三陸海岸の岩を模したアプローチゲート).
- **Sign:** the JR East green 「JR 気仙沼駅 Kesennuma Station」 board over the arcade. There is also a blue JR vertical
  sign post.
- **Platforms:**
  - An **island platform, 1 face 2 tracks**, for the 大船渡線 (Ichinoseki line). Platform 3 is a cut-in (切欠き) bay
    toward 新月.
  - The **BRT platforms (bays 1–2)** lie between the station building and the rail platform, at level with it.
  - The platform canopy is light blue on the ortho (colour read by eye, not sampled).
- **Station square:** a taxi rank, a **small observation pavilion and a monument**, and the 「ようこそ気仙沼へ！」 sign
  tower (OSM man_made=tower node at 38.90936, 141.55889). There are also **fish sculptures** (a カジキマグロ and three
  カツオ; OSM tourism=artwork nodes) and the 気仙沼市観光案内所.

## Colours

Walls #e2ddd3, arch trim #cfc9bd, roof #4f5763; the mural is dark blue-black with white, red and orange figures; the JR
sign is green #2e8b57 with white letters.

## Current state

- Rail: 大船渡線 to 一ノ関.
- BRT: 気仙沼線 BRT and 大船渡線 BRT buses (Commons shows an Isuzu Erga MIO on the line).
- It is the only railway station in the city.
