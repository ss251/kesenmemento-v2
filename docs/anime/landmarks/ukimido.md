# 浮見堂 (Ukimidō), 神明崎

## Position

| Point | lat, lon | ENU (x, z) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pavilion (centre of the roof square on the ortho) | 38.90621, 141.57894 | (341.6, −23.4) | GSI ortho; equals the app's `SPOTS.ukimido` (0 m error) |

- The pavilion stands at the **southern tip of 神明崎**, on the line of the tip's seawall. About half of the square sits on
  the rock or seawall edge, and the rest projects over the water on piles.
- The DEM at the tip is 3.5 m.
- Address of the 神明崎 area: 気仙沼市魚町2丁目6-7 (the tourism association; GSI geocode 38.90746, 141.57884).

## Dimensions and structure

- **Pavilion.** A small open **四阿 (azumaya)** with a square plan.
  - The roof seen on the ortho is about **6.5 to 7 m square** including the eaves, so the posts are about 4.5 to 5 m apart.
  - Pyramid roof (宝形造) with a finial.
  - Painted vermilion (朱塗り) timber: posts, beams and railings.
- **浮見海道 (the red walkway).** A railed promenade built **over the sea around the perimeter of 神明崎**.
  - The rails are vermilion. This walkway is the 「海上に作られた赤い遊歩道」 of the city page.
  - It runs from the west-side quay around the tip past the pavilion and up the east side. The ortho shows it as a thin
    red-brown band hugging the rocks, about 2 to 2.5 m wide.
  - The inner-bay scale model displayed in PIER7 (raw/photos IMG_0582, IMG_0598) shows the same **red rim around the
    whole tip** of the peninsula.
  - It is **not** a straight pier from the shore to a pavilion standing out in the sea.
- History: built in **1932 (昭和7年) by young volunteers of 魚町**, and rebuilt between June 2019 and **May 2020
  (令和2年)**.
- Lighting: lit up **from sunset to 22:00**.
- Nearby: the **3rd-generation standing 恵比寿像 (2020)** holding a bonito (カツオ) instead of a sea bream, right foot
  stepping forward. It is 「全国でも珍しい立ち恵比寿像」.
  - OSM node 恵比寿像 is at 38.90620, 141.57914, that is ENU (359, −22), 17 m east of the pavilion on the tip's east side.

## Colours

- Vermilion 朱 #d2462f (photo references; lit side #e05a3a).
- Roof: grey metal or tile. The ortho median of the roof is #a9c6bd (cyan cast) → **#9aa3a2, a light grey**. It is
  **not** a dark tile roof.
- Deck: timber #a98464. Piles: concrete #b9b6ad.
- Night: warm floodlight from below the eaves.

## Signage

A plaque 浮見堂 under the eave (existing app detail; plausible, not verified from photos). A tourism board
「神明崎 浮見堂」 stands at the entrance.
