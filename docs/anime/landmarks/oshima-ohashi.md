# 気仙沼大島大橋 (Kesennuma Oshima Bridge, nickname 鶴亀大橋)

- The OSM way is **356.0 m** long, which equals the official total length.
- Bearing in the app convention (`atan2(dz, dx)`): **108.2°**, about 18° west of south going south.
- The DEM gives the mainland abutment rock about 26 m high, and the island end falls to the sea.
- The strait (大島瀬戸) is narrowest here.

## Dimensions and structure

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Type | steel **mid-deck arch** (鋼中路式アーチ橋); the deck passes through the ribs | Miyagi Prefecture; 大日本ダイヤコンサルタント |
| Total length | 356.0 m | pref.; dd-con |
| Spans | 24.7 + 40.5 + **224.0** + 40.5 + 24.7 m | dd-con |
| Arch span | **297 m**, the longest arch bridge in eastern Japan | pref. |
| Arch rise | **54.0 m** | dd-con |
| Deck width | **9.5 m**: 2.50 m walkway + 3.50 m + 3.50 m lanes | dd-con; Wikipedia |
| Clearance | **≥ 32 m** | Wikipedia |
| Road | 宮城県道218号 大島浪板線, 2 lanes plus a sidewalk | OSM; Wikipedia |
| Opened | 2019-04-07 15:00 (the girder was erected by 2017) | Wikipedia; pref. |
| Lighting | lights in the railings, at the bridge ends (袂) and inside the girder, completed July 2018 | Wikipedia |

Form, from the photos:

- Two parallel arch ribs joined by a few horizontal **portal struts** near the crown (ladder bracing), with no X-bracing.
- They spring from the rock slopes on both shores a few metres above the sea.
- The deck crosses the ribs at about 1/3 of the rise from each springing.
- Thin vertical hangers carry the central 224 m part, and posts carry the ends.

## Colours

- The design is **white as the main colour**, to suit the green island. Ribs, hangers, girder and railings are #eeefec
  (shade #c8ccd0). Deck asphalt #5f6368. The walkway is on the east side, with white railings of vertical bars.

## Current state

Open since April 2019. It replaced the 大島汽船 ferry (see `oshima-ferry-pier.md`).
